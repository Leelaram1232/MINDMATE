MINDMATE NER — Caregiver Guide

How to support a loved one with MindMate.

1. Set up your account

Sign up as a caregiver, add your phone number, and choose the language for the app and insights.

  1. Tap Get Started and create a caregiver account.
  2. Save your call-back number in Settings so they can call you from Mira.
  3. Download extra languages if you want the portal and insights in Hindi, Telugu, or Tamil.

2. Link a patient

A one-time invite code connects your dashboard to their games, reminders, and activity.

  1. Open Patients and generate an invite code.
  2. Ask them to enter that code on their Home screen.
  3. When they are linked, pick their name on the dashboard.

3. Watch wellbeing

Overview, Activity, Cognitive Progress, and Reminders show how they are doing this week.

  1. Use Overview for games played, accuracy, and reminder completion.
  2. Open Cognitive Progress for weekly trends.
  3. Add or check reminders so daily tasks stay visible on their phone.

4. Stay reachable

Keep your phone number current. If they ask Mira to call their caregiver, MindMate opens this number.

  1. Update your number any time in Settings.
  2. Encourage a short game in the morning if scores drop.
  3. MindMate is a wellness companion, not a medical diagnosis tool.

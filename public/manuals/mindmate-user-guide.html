MINDMATE NER — User Guide

A simple guide for people using MindMate every day.

1. Get started

Create your account, choose “I am using MindMate”, and pick the language you want the app and Mira to use.

  1. Tap Get Started, then create an account or sign in.
  2. Choose your language. If it is not ready, tap it to download the voice pack.
  3. On Home you will see games, reminders, progress, and Talk with Mira.

2. Play games

Short games help keep memory and attention active. Mira suggests an easy or brighter pace from your recent scores.

  1. Open Games from Home or the bottom bar.
  2. Choose Memory Match, Pattern Recall, or Object Recognition.
  3. Play one short round. Your score is saved so caregivers can see progress.

3. Reminders and progress

Reminders help with medicine, water, and daily activities. Progress shows how often you played.

  1. Open Reminders and mark a task done when you finish it.
  2. Open My Progress to see your streak and accuracy.
  3. Ask a caregiver for an invite code on Home if you want them to stay connected.

4. Talk with Mira

Mira is your companion. She answers in your chosen language and can open games, reminders, or call your caregiver.

  1. Tap the microphone on Home, or open Voice Assistance.
  2. Tap to hear Mira, then speak or type a short message.
  3. Say “call my caregiver” if they have saved a phone number.
